Career & Promotions

Find your MOS cutoff, total your promotion points, and see how close you are to the current SGT/SSG cutoff. SoldierTools separates your personal points from the Army's MOS cutoff so one does not get confused for the other.

Accuracy rule: Promotion cutoffs are monthly HQDA/HRC data and can change every month. SoldierTools never treats an old cutoff as a permanent requirement. Your official promotion-point record remains IPPS-A. Open HRC → IPPS-A policy →

1. My Promotion Points

Enter the point categories shown on your current Promotion Point Worksheet. SoldierTools totals them without pretending it can see your IPPS-A record.

2. MOS Cutoff Lookup

Search the MOS you care about. This prototype includes a verified HQDA snapshot and identifies the data month so a stale number cannot silently look current.

3. Promotion Outlook

Put your estimated/validated point total against the MOS cutoff. A positive margin means you are above the published cutoff; it does not guarantee promotion.

Standards & Regulations

The SoldierTools quick-reference hub for the standards Soldiers actually get corrected on. Use the summaries to learn what right looks like, then verify the exact requirement in the current Army publication, Army directive, and your command policy.

Important: Army policies change. SoldierTools summarizes official guidance for quick study; it does not replace a current regulation, directive, command policy, or legal advice. The Army's official uniform page currently points Soldiers to AR 670-1, DA Pam 670-1, AR 600-8-22 and current appearance directives. Verify current Army guidance.

Grooming

Hair, Mustache & Sideburns

Know the current grooming rules instead of relying on “my old NCO said…”

  • Male Soldiers must remain clean-shaven while in uniform/on duty unless an authorized exception applies.
  • Mustaches are authorized when maintained within Army standards.
  • Sideburns must be trimmed according to current guidance.
  • Hair must present a professional appearance and comply with the current directive.
Current Army policy updates →
Uniform Maintenance

Keep Your Uniform Right

Simple habits that prevent most uniform corrections.

  • Keep uniforms clean, serviceable and properly fitted.
  • Do not alter the basic design or appearance of regulated uniform items.
  • Replace damaged or unserviceable items through authorized channels.
  • Verify commercial items are actually authorized before wearing them.
Insignia

Rank, Name & Unit Items

Know what goes where before formation, inspection or board.

  • Know the correct placement for rank and name tapes.
  • Know authorized SSI, combat/service patches, tabs and badges.
  • Do not mix unauthorized or obsolete insignia into a prescribed uniform.
  • Use DA Pam 670-1 for detailed placement diagrams.
Personal Appearance

Nails, Jewelry & Accessories

Small details are common inspection corrections.

  • Keep fingernails within current length and appearance standards.
  • Wear only authorized jewelry/accessories with the prescribed uniform.
  • Check current directive language before relying on an old pocket guide.
Tattoos & Body Modifications

Know the Limits

Tattoo and body-modification policy has changed over time, so use current guidance.

  • Do not rely on old tattoo charts or social-media posts.
  • Check the current AR 670-1/DA Pam 670-1 and Army directives.
  • When in doubt, ask your chain of command or G-1.

Leader's Quick Check

Before Inspection

  • Uniform clean, serviceable and correctly fitted
  • Correct rank, name, patches, badges and tabs
  • Boots/footwear clean and serviceable
  • Hair, mustache, sideburns and nails within current standards
  • Required headgear and seasonal items present
  • No unauthorized items or alterations
  • Check local command policy for stricter/mission-specific requirements
Official references: Army Uniforms & Grooming and Tell Your Formation — Policy Updates. Current policy includes Army Directive 2025-18 for appearance/grooming/uniform standards and newer facial-hair guidance; always check the latest directive.

AFT Performance Calculator

Enter your actual performance. SoldierTools converts each event to points using the Army's AFT scoring tables effective June 1, 2025.

Select the aerobic event you are using. Alternate-event eligibility and scoring must be verified against current Army guidance and your authorized profile.

The Army AFT has five events: 3RM deadlift, hand-release push-up, sprint-drag-carry, plank and 2-mile run. The general standard is sex/age-normed; the combat standard is sex-neutral and age-normed. Combat specialties require at least 60 points in each event and 350 overall; the general standard requires at least 60 points in each event and 300 overall. Verify your official score with your unit/testing personnel.

Source: U.S. Army AFT Scoring Scales, approved May 15, 2025; effective June 1, 2025.
